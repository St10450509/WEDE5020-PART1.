About Thorne & Co.

Our Story

Thorne & Co. Plumbing Services was founded in 2015 by qualified plumber Marcus Thorne. What began as a one-person operation servicing residential clients in the Randburg area has since grown into a trusted team of four plumbers offering both residential and small-commercial services across the greater Johannesburg area.


Over the years, word of mouth and a Facebook page full of happy customers have kept the business growing. This website is the next step: giving our clients an easy way to learn about our services, see our past work, and book us online.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To provide reliable, honest, and high-quality plumbing services that give clients peace of mind.
